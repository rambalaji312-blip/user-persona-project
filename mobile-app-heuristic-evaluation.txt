MOBILE APP HEURISTIC EVALUATION

App Evaluated:
Food Delivery Mobile App

1. Visibility of System Status
The app shows order status clearly.

2. Match Between System and Real World
Simple language is used.

3. User Control and Freedom
Users can cancel orders easily.

4. Consistency and Standards
Icons and buttons are consistent.

5. Error Prevention
Confirmation appears before payment.

6. Recognition Rather Than Recall
Menus are easy to find.

7. Flexibility and Efficiency
Search feature helps users quickly.

8. Aesthetic and Minimalist Design
Interface is clean and simple.

Conclusion:
The app provides a good user experience with only minor improvements needed.
