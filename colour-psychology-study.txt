COLOR PSYCHOLOGY STUDY

Introduction:
Color psychology studies how colors influence human emotions and behavior.

Red:
- Represents energy, passion and excitement.
- Often used in sales and promotions.

Blue:
- Represents trust, calmness and reliability.
- Commonly used by banks and technology companies.

Green:
- Represents nature, health and growth.
- Frequently used in healthcare and environmental brands.

Yellow:
- Represents happiness, optimism and creativity.
- Used to attract attention.

Black:
- Represents power, elegance and sophistication.
- Popular in luxury brands.

Conclusion:
Colors play an important role in user experience and branding because they affect how users feel and respond.
